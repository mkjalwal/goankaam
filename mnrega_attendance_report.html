Part 1: MGNREGA (मनरेगा) Government Work & Digital Attendance Portal

1. Purpose & Administrative Scope

The MGNREGA portal within GaonKaam provides Gram Rozgar Sevaks, Panchayat Secretaries, and village job-card holders with an automated muster roll management dashboard. It complies with the Ministry of Rural Development's National Mobile Monitoring System (NMMS) standards to ensure transparency, prevent ghost worker fraud, and accelerate Direct Benefit Transfer (DBT) wage disbursements.

  • Village-Scoped Filtering: Automatically isolates government projects and registered job-card laborers according to the active village selected (e.g., Sundarpur, Block Robertsganj).
  • Work Types Supported: Amrit Sarovar Pond excavation, rural link roads, plantation and horticulture, flood-control embankment construction, and canal de-silting.
  • Live Muster Roll Calculations: Instant tallying of present workers, absent workers, and total daily wage liability based on the official sanctioned rate (₹260 / day).

2. Dynamic Wage Accumulator Formula

Wage Accumulation Engine:
$$\text{Total Wages Due} = \left(\sum \text{Days marked } [P]\right) \times \text{Agreed Daily Wage Rate } (₹)$$ Example: Ramesh Mistri (Mason) → 6 Days Present @ ₹600/day = ₹3,600 Total Due.

Part 3: Worker Perspective & Direct WhatsApp Audit Slips

1. Transparent Worker Dashboard

When a worker logs into GaonKaam with the Worker role, their profile shifts from recruitment discovery to personal livelihood tracking:

  • Active Engagements: Displays which village employer currently holds their active contract.
  • Personal Daily Log: Shows the exact attendance pills marked by the work owner.
  • Earnings Counter: Live calculation of their wages to date, eliminating arbitrary wage deductions at project completion.
